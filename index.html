Mercedes Traditional Festival

Three days of food, music, tradition and celebration in Mercedes, Buenos Aires.

About the Event

Every year, Mercedes celebrates one of its most beloved traditions: the Salame Quintero. This festival brings together local flavors, music, culture and people from the city and beyond. In 2026, the celebration returns for its 51st edition, inviting visitors to enjoy three days full of tradition, entertainment and unforgettable moments.

The 2026 Edition

Date

September 11, 12 and 13, 2026

Location

Mercedes, Buenos Aires, Argentina

For three days, Mercedes becomes a meeting point for locals and visitors who come together to celebrate food, music and local traditions. From Friday evening until Sunday, the festival offers a different experience each day, with live performances and activities for the whole family.

What to Expect

The Mercedes Traditional Festival is much more than a music concert. It is a celebration of Mercedes and its traditions. Visitors can discover local food, enjoy live music and experience the warm atmosphere of a traditional Argentine festival.

Local flavors

One of the main attractions of the festival is, of course, the famous Salame Quintero. This traditional product is closely connected to the identity and history of Mercedes.

Music and Entertainment

Across the three days, different artists and groups will take the stage, creating a varied musical program for visitors of all ages.

Tradition and Community

The festival is also a celebration of local identity. Families, friends, producers, artists and visitors come together to share food, music and traditions.

Tips

Keep these recommendations in mind to make the most of your festival experience.

  • Check the schedule before arriving.
  • Arrive early if you want to enjoy the festival from the beginning.
  • Bring comfortable clothes and shoes.
  • Leave enough time to explore the food and festival areas.
  • Bring cash as well as a payment method for different vendors.
  • Come ready to enjoy music, local food and Argentine traditions.

The Soundtrack of the Festival

Music is an essential part of the Fiesta Nacional del Salame Quintero. Before arriving in Mercedes, get into the festival spirit with a playlist inspired by the artists, Argentine popular music and the atmosphere of the celebration.

How to Get There

Located not far from Buenos Aires City, Mercedes is an accessible destination for visitors looking for a traditional Argentine experience outside the capital.
